3. What other people can see

  • The pledge count and the cities and countries of recent pledges are public.
  • Your personal pledge page shows your name, city, country, pledge number and date. It has a private, hard-to-guess link that search engines do not list.
  • A Pledge wall line is shown with your first name and city once it passes our checks.
  • Community profiles (name, username, city, about me, photo, follower counts), posts, photos and comments are public. Do not post anything you want to keep private.
  • Private messages are seen only by the two people in the chat (see the Terms of Use, section 8).
  • Your email, phone number, complaints and payment details are never shown publicly.

4. Who we share data with

  • Service providers (Data Processors) who work for us under contract and only on our instructions: our hosting provider, which runs the server where the data is kept (the server may be in or outside India), and, if switched on, Google for ads (see the Cookie Policy).
  • Services your browser contacts when a page loads: Unsplash (home page photos) and Google Fonts (one script font). They can see your internet address under their own privacy policies.
  • Authorities: a court, the police or a government agency, only when the law requires, for example under section 69, 69A or 79 of the IT Act, the IT Rules, the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 or the DPDP Act. Where the IT Rules require, we may share the identity of the person who posted unlawful content with the victim who complains.
  • Tax authorities, for referral payouts and advertising income, as the Income-tax and GST laws require.
  • If the initiative is handed to another person or body to continue it, your data will go with it under this same policy, and we will tell you.

Where data is processed outside India, we do so only to countries not restricted by the Government of India under section 16 of the DPDP Act.

6. How we protect it

We use reasonable security practices as required by section 8(5) of the DPDP Act, rule 6 of the DPDP Rules and rule 8 of the SPDI Rules, including: an encrypted (HTTPS) connection for every page; passwords stored only as one-way scrambled codes; internet addresses stored only in coded form; removal of hidden photo data; an owner page protected by a password; access to the server limited to the owner; regular backups, kept on the server and as private copies downloaded only by the owner, in which login sessions are removed and old copies are deleted after 30 days; and checks against robots and spam. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password that you do not use elsewhere.

If there is a data breach, we will tell each affected person without delay, in plain language, what happened, when, the likely effect on them, what we are doing about it, what they can do to stay safe, and whom to contact. We will report it to the Data Protection Board of India and, where needed, to CERT-In, within the times the law sets (an initial report to the Board without delay, and a detailed report within 72 hours).

7. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you have the right to:

  • know what personal data of yours we hold, how we use it, and with whom we shared it (section 11);
  • correct, complete, update or erase your personal data (section 12). Community members can edit their profile and delete posts, messages or the whole account themselves at any time;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, as easily as you gave it (section 6(4)). This does not affect what was done before. If you withdraw consent, we will stop using that data and erase it, unless the law requires us to keep it;
  • complain to our Grievance Officer, and if you are not satisfied, to the Data Protection Board of India (section 13);
  • nominate a person to use your rights if you die or become unable to act (section 14).

To use a right, use the complaint form and choose "Personal data request", or write to the email above. For a pledge, tell us the city and approximate date. We may ask you to confirm who you are. We will reply as soon as possible and within 30 days, and never later than the 90 days allowed by the DPDP Rules.

Your duties (section 15 of the DPDP Act): give true information, do not pretend to be another person, and do not make false or frivolous complaints. Breaking these duties can lead to a penalty of up to ₹10,000.

8. Children

Under the DPDP Act a "child" is a person under 18. Q India Community is only for people aged 18 or older, and we close accounts that we learn belong to a child. A child may take the pledge only with a parent's or lawful guardian's consent and help, giving only a city and no name, email or phone. If we learn that we hold a child's personal data without verifiable consent of a parent or guardian, we will erase it. We do not track children, watch their behaviour, or show them targeted ads (section 9 of the DPDP Act). Parents can ask us to erase a child's data through the complaint form.

9. Cookies and device storage

See the Cookie Policy. In short: we use no analytics and no tracking cookies of our own. The Website stores your language, your pledge link and your login on your own device. Google ads, if switched on, may use cookies.

10. Changes

We will update this policy when our practices or the law change and show the new date at the top. If a change means we want to use your data for a new purpose, we will ask for your consent again.
