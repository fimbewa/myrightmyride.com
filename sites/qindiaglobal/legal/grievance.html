Grievance Redressal

Last updated: 26 November 2026. Version 1.0.

This page sets out our grievance redressal mechanism under rule 3(2) of the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021 (the "IT Rules"), as amended up to 2026, and under section 13 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and rule 14 of the DPDP Rules, 2025.

Who can complain

Any user or victim, or a person acting on their behalf, about anything on qindiaglobal.com: content that breaks our Terms or the law, a member's behaviour, the use of your personal data, copying of your work, or our decision to remove your content or suspend your account.

How to complain

  • Online form (best): below. You get a complaint number at once; that is our acknowledgement.
  • Report button on any Community post, comment, profile or message.
  • Email or post to the Grievance Officer at the address above. Write "Grievance" in the subject.

Please give: your name and an email or phone to reply to; the link (web address) of the content, or the username; what is wrong and why; and, for copyright, the details listed in the Copyright Policy.

Our time limits

Type of complaintTime limit
Acknowledgement of any complaintWithin 24 hours (the complaint number shown on screen; we also reply by email or phone)
Content that shows a person's private area, full or partial nudity, or a sexual act, or that impersonates a person, including morphed or AI-made images, reported by that person or someone on their behalfRemoved or access disabled within 2 hours of the complaint
Other request to remove unlawful content listed in rule 3(1)(b) of the IT RulesActed on as fast as possible and resolved within 36 hours
Order of a court or notice of the appropriate government or its authorised agencyWithin the time set by law, currently 3 hours
All other complaints, including appeals against our own decisionsResolved within 7 days
Requests about your personal data (see, correct, erase, withdraw consent)As soon as possible and within 30 days (never later than 90 days under the DPDP Rules)

We will tell you what we decided and why. If we remove a member's content because of a complaint, we will, where possible, tell that member and let them ask for a review.

If you are not satisfied

  • Grievance Appellate Committee. If you are not satisfied with the Grievance Officer's decision, or get no decision in time, you may appeal to the Grievance Appellate Committee set up by the Government of India under rule 3A of the IT Rules, within 30 days, online at gac.gov.in.
  • Data Protection Board of India. For complaints about your personal data, after using our grievance process, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India under section 13(3) of the DPDP Act.
  • Police and courts. You may at any time report a crime to the police or the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in, helpline 1930), or go to a court.
