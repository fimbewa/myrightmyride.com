Invitation Rules (no referral fee)

Last updated: 26 November 2026. Version 2.0.

No fee, reward or payment for referrals. Qindiaglobal does not pay any referral fee, reward, commission, bonus, prize or share of income to anyone for inviting people or for sign-ups through an invite link. Inviting friends is free, voluntary and only for spreading the cause.

Why your invitation matters

  • Habits spread from person to person. People queue when they see others they know doing it. Every friend you bring is one more calm example at a station door or bus stop.
  • Your invitation is the movement. Qindiaglobal has no government backing and no ad budget. It grows only because ordinary people say to their family, friends and colleagues: "Join me, let us queue up with dignity."
  • Safer journeys for those who need it most. Every new member who takes the pledge makes travel a little safer for children, older people, women and people with disabilities.
  • The reward is a better India. We invite others not for money but for pride in our cities, our trains and our buses, and for a country that boards with dignity.

Invite one friend today. Then ask them to invite one more.

Members of Q India Community can share their personal invite link (qindiaglobal.com/community?ref=username) so friends can join. The link only shows who invited whom. These rules are part of our Terms of Use.

1. No referral fee or reward

  • No money, gift, discount, credit or other benefit is paid or promised for invitations or sign-ups, however many people join through your link.
  • Nobody may claim a referral fee or reward from Qindiaglobal, its owner or its volunteers.
  • For a few hours on 26 November 2026 an earlier version of this page described a planned reward. That plan was withdrawn on the same day, before anyone could qualify. No reward was ever earned or paid under it, and none is or will become due.
  • If this ever changes, it will happen only through a new written announcement on this page. Nothing said by anyone else, on the website or elsewhere, creates a right to a reward.

2. How you may invite people

  • Share your link with people you know, or on your own social media, honestly.
  • Do not send spam, bulk or unsolicited SMS, calls, emails or WhatsApp messages, and do not post your link in other people's comments or groups without permission. Bulk commercial messages are also controlled by the Telecom Commercial Communications Customer Preference Regulations, 2018.
  • Do not offer money or gifts to people to sign up, do not promise them earnings, and do not say that Qindiaglobal is a government scheme, a job or a way to make money.
  • Do not make fake, extra or robot accounts. We may remove them and close the accounts involved.

3. Questions

If anyone asks you for money or promises you money in the name of Qindiaglobal, do not pay, and tell the Grievance Officer. Personal data is handled under the Privacy Policy.
