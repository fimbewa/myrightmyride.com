Referral Program Terms

Last updated: 26 November 2026. Version 1.0.

The referral reward is being finalised. The exact reward, how it is calculated and when it is paid will be announced on the Community page and added here before any reward is paid. These terms apply from today to every invitation.

Members of Q India Community can invite others with their personal invite link (qindiaglobal.com/community?ref=username). These terms are part of our Terms of Use.

1. Who can take part

  • Any member aged 18 or older whose account is in good standing.
  • To receive a reward in money, the member must be resident in India and must give a valid PAN and a UPI ID or Indian bank account in their own name. Owners, volunteers who run the Website, and their close family may take part but may not receive money rewards.

2. What counts as a qualifying sign-up

  • A new, real person who joins Q India Community through your invite link, is 18 or older, and has not had an account before.
  • The account must still be active and not blocked or deleted when the reward is calculated.
  • Your own extra accounts, fake, bought or robot accounts, and accounts made with false details do not count.
  • A reward becomes due only when you reach the number of qualifying sign-ups announced for the program (currently 1,000).
  • Rewards never depend on anyone clicking or viewing ads.

3. How you may invite people

  • Share your link with people you know, or on your own social media, honestly.
  • Do not send spam, bulk or unsolicited SMS, calls, emails or WhatsApp messages, and do not post your link in other people's comments or groups without permission. Bulk commercial messages are also controlled by the Telecom Commercial Communications Customer Preference Regulations, 2018.
  • Do not offer money or gifts to people to sign up, do not promise them earnings, and do not say that Qindiaglobal is a government scheme or a job.

4. Fraud and our checks

We check sign-ups for fraud. If we find fake sign-ups, spam or any breach of these terms, we may cancel the sign-ups, withhold or recover a reward, and close the account. Our decision on whether a sign-up qualifies is final, subject to your right to complain to the Grievance Officer.

5. Reward and payment

  • The reward is a thank-you for helping the initiative grow. It is not a salary, not employment, not a lottery and not a prize competition, and no payment is ever needed to take part.
  • Rewards are paid by UPI or bank transfer to an account in your own name, within the time announced. We will never ask you to pay anything to receive a reward.

6. Tax

  • A referral reward is income in your hands and you are responsible for paying any tax on it.
  • Where the law requires, we will deduct tax at source (TDS) before paying, for example on commission or on business benefits and perquisites under section 393 of the Income-tax Act, 2025 (these replaced sections 194H and 194R of the Income-tax Act, 1961 from 1 April 2026), and give you a TDS certificate.
  • We need your PAN before paying. Without a valid PAN, tax may be deducted at a higher rate.

7. Changes and ending the program

We may change or end the program with notice on the Website. Rewards already earned under the rules at that time will be paid. Personal data given for payouts is handled under the Privacy Policy.
