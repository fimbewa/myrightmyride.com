Cookie Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026. Version 1.0.

Cookies are small files that a website saves in your browser. "Local storage" is a similar store in the browser. This policy explains what qindiaglobal.com stores on your device. Read it with the Privacy Policy.

What we store ourselves

We use no analytics and no tracking cookies of our own. We store only what is strictly needed for the features you use:

NameTypePurposeHow long
qi_langLocal storageRemembers the language you choseUntil you clear it
qi_slugLocal storageRemembers the link to your own pledge pageUntil you clear it
qi_s_tokenLocal storageKeeps you logged in to Q India CommunityUntil you log out
qi_s_refLocal storageRemembers who invited you, until you sign upUntil you sign up or clear it
qi_ownerSession storageKeeps the owner logged in to the owner pageUntil the browser tab is closed

These stay on your device and are not used to track you. Because they are strictly necessary for the service you asked for, they do not need separate consent.

Ads

  • Our own ads (from businesses that advertise with us directly) use no cookies and no tracking. We count only the total number of times each ad is seen and tapped.
  • Google ads. Some ad spaces may show ads from Google AdSense, if the owner switches them on. Google and its partners may then use cookies or similar technology to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. Visitors in Europe and the UK are asked for consent first. You can turn off personalised ads at adssettings.google.com, and learn more at How Google uses information from sites that use its services. Google ads are not shown in a way that targets children.

Other services

Home page photos load from Unsplash and one script font from Google Fonts. Share buttons open WhatsApp, Facebook, X or Telegram only when you tap them. Those services have their own cookie and privacy policies.

How to control cookies

You can delete or block cookies and local storage in your browser settings. If you block what we store, the Website still works, but it may forget your language and you will need to log in again.
