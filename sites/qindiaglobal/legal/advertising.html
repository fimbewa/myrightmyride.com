Advertising Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026. Version 1.0.

Ads help pay for posters, awareness drives and the Website, so that every feature stays free for users. This policy applies to every advertiser and to members who are paid to promote anything on the Website. It is part of our Terms of Use.

1. How ads appear

  • Every ad is clearly marked "Ad" or "Sponsored" and is easy to tell apart from posts and from our own content.
  • An ad does not mean that Qindiaglobal recommends the product, and never means that any government body or transport operator supports the advertiser.
  • Our own ads are not targeted at individuals and use no tracking. Google ads, if switched on, work as described in the Cookie Policy.
  • We do not show ads designed for children, and we do not target ads at children.

2. Rules every ad must follow

Every ad must be legal, decent, honest and truthful, and must follow:

  • the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, including the ban on misleading advertisements (sections 2(28), 21 and 89);
  • the Guidelines for Prevention of Misleading Advertisements and Endorsements for Misleading Advertisements, 2022, and the Guidelines for Prevention and Regulation of Dark Patterns, 2023, issued by the Central Consumer Protection Authority;
  • the Code for Self-Regulation of Advertising Content in India of the Advertising Standards Council of India (ASCI), and its guidelines, including those for influencer advertising;
  • any law that applies to the product, such as the Drugs and Magic Remedies (Objectionable Advertisements) Act, 1954, the Cigarettes and Other Tobacco Products Act, 2003, the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006, the Legal Metrology Act, 2009, the Emblems and Names (Prevention of Improper Use) Act, 1950, the Indecent Representation of Women (Prohibition) Act, 1986, and the Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Act, 2025.

Claims must be true and supported by proof that the advertiser gives us on request. Prices must include all charges. Offers must state their conditions clearly. Ads must not use false urgency, hidden costs, "bait and switch", disguised ads or other dark patterns.

3. Ads we never accept

  • Political, election or religious ads.
  • Adult, sexual or dating ads.
  • Gambling, betting, lotteries and online money games, and ads for any service that offers them, including surrogate ads.
  • Alcohol, tobacco, e-cigarettes, drugs, and surrogate ads for any of these.
  • Prescription medicines, "magic" cures, and products claiming to cure diseases.
  • Weapons, explosives, and anything illegal.
  • Crypto-assets and other virtual digital assets, unregistered investment or deposit schemes, chit funds without registration, pyramid or multi-level money schemes, "get rich quick" or loan offers from unregistered lenders.
  • Ads that use the national emblem, any government name or logo, or that suggest government or transport-operator approval.
  • Ads that are misleading, hateful, shocking, or that make fun of people who queue or do not queue.

4. Our review and right to refuse

We check every ad before it goes live and may refuse, pause or remove any ad at any time, including after it is live, if we believe it breaks this policy or the law, or if a complaint is received. Advertisers can report problems or appeal our decisions to the Grievance Officer (see Grievance Redressal).

5. Price, payment, tax and refunds

  • The price and dates are agreed in writing (email or message) before the ad runs.
  • Payment is made by UPI or bank transfer to the account the owner gives in writing. We will never ask for payment through any other person, link or account.
  • GST and other taxes are charged where applicable, and a receipt or tax invoice is given.
  • If we refuse an ad before it runs, we refund the full amount. If we cannot run a paid ad for all agreed days, or remove it for a reason that is not the advertiser's fault, we refund the unused days in proportion. Refunds are made within 15 days to the account the payment came from. No refund is due if an ad is removed because it broke this policy or the law.

6. Advertiser's promises

The advertiser confirms that it has every right needed for the ad (text, images, trade marks, music, people's consent), that the ad and the product follow the law, and that it is responsible for its own ads, offers, products and websites. The advertiser will make good any loss, claim or penalty that Qindiaglobal suffers because of its ad.

7. Paid promotion by members

A member who is paid, or given any product, benefit or relationship, for a post must say so clearly at the start of the post (for example "#ad", "Sponsored" or "Paid partnership"), as the CCPA endorsement guidelines and ASCI guidelines require. Undisclosed paid posts, and any political or banned-category promotion, will be removed.

8. Contact

To advertise, use the Advertise page. For complaints about an ad, use the complaint form or contact the Grievance Officer at vijaybadgujar999@gmail.com.
