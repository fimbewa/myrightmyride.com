Community Guidelines

Last updated: 26 November 2026. Version 1.0.

Q India Community is a place for citizens who believe in queuing up and boarding with dignity. These guidelines explain our Terms of Use in plain words. They apply to profiles, usernames, posts, photos, comments, Pledge wall lines and private messages.

Please do

  • Share your experiences, tips, photos and good examples of orderly queues and polite travel.
  • Disagree politely. Criticise ideas, not people.
  • Post only photos you took yourself or have the right to share. Hide the faces of strangers, and never post photos of children without their parent's consent.
  • Say clearly when an image is AI-made or edited to look real.
  • Say clearly when you are paid or given anything for a post (write "#ad" or "Paid partnership" at the start), as the advertising rules require.
  • Report anything that breaks these rules with the Report button, and use Block for people who bother you.

If someone is in immediate danger, call 112. To report cyber crime, call 1930 or go to cybercrime.gov.in. If you or someone you know is thinking of self-harm, call the Tele-MANAS helpline 14416.

What happens if rules are broken

  1. Removal or hiding of the content. Content reported by three members is hidden until we check it.
  2. Warning for a first, minor breach.
  3. Suspension of the account for repeated or serious breaches.
  4. Permanent ban straight away for sexual content involving children, non-consensual intimate images, credible threats, terror content, fraud or deepfakes of real people.
  5. Reporting to the police or other authorities where the content may be a crime, as the law requires. Removed content is kept for 180 days for investigation.

If you think we made a mistake, you can ask for a review or appeal. See Grievance Redressal.
