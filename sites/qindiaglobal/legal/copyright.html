Copyright and Takedown Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026. Version 1.0.

We respect the rights of creators and expect members to do the same. This policy follows the Copyright Act, 1957 (section 52(1)(c)), rule 75 of the Copyright Rules, 2013, the Trade Marks Act, 1999 and the IT Rules, 2021.

1. If someone copied your work

Send a written notice to the Grievance Officer through the complaint form (choose "Copyright or trade mark") or by email, with:

  1. your name, address and email or phone, and whether you are the owner or authorised by the owner;
  2. a description of your work (and, for a trade mark, its registration number) and proof of your rights;
  3. the exact link of the copy on qindiaglobal.com;
  4. why it is not a fair use or other permitted use;
  5. if you know it, who posted it;
  6. a statement that the information is true and that you are the owner or authorised to act.

2. What we do

  • We acknowledge the notice within 24 hours.
  • If the notice is complete, we disable access to the copy as soon as possible and within 36 hours, and tell the member who posted it.
  • As rule 75 of the Copyright Rules, 2013 provides, where the access was disabled under section 52(1)(c), we may restore the content if the complainant does not give us an order of a competent court within 21 days.
  • A court order is always followed.

3. If your content was removed

If you believe your content was removed by mistake, or you have the right to post it, send a counter-notice through the complaint form (choose "Appeal") with your details, the link, and why you have the right to post it. We will share it with the complainant and decide within 7 days.

4. Repeat infringers

We close the accounts of members who repeatedly post others' work without permission.

5. Our own content

The Qindiaglobal name, logo, posters, translations and text are the initiative's work. You may share links and print the free posters unchanged for non-commercial use, as the Terms of Use explain. For any other use, write to vijaybadgujar999@gmail.com.
