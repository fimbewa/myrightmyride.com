Careers at Qindiaglobal

Join the people who are making "queue up, board with dignity" a habit across India and the world.

Every role here is voluntary and unpaid. You give a little time; millions of travellers get a safer, calmer journey.

See the roles Apply now

Why joining matters

  • Pushing at doors hurts people. Every day, passengers fall, get crushed or miss their stop because of rushing at train, metro and bus doors. Children, older people, women and people with disabilities suffer most.
  • A queue is the cheapest safety system there is. It needs no money, no machine and no new law, only people who choose to wait their turn and show others how.
  • Habits change when ordinary people lead. Signs and announcements help, but commuters follow what they see others do. One calm volunteer at a busy gate can change how hundreds of people board every day.
  • Orderly boarding saves time for everyone. When people let others get off first and then board in line, doors clear faster and trains and buses leave on time.
  • It is about dignity. We are a great nation and we deserve to travel like one. Every person who joins proves that discipline is something we choose, not something forced on us.

What you get

  • Real experience in community work, public speaking, writing and leadership that you can add to your CV.
  • A volunteer certificate from Qindiaglobal on request after 3 months of active work.
  • Your name with your stories and photos on the Q India blog (Reporters), and a place in a growing network of people who care about their city.
  • The pride of seeing your station, bus stop or campus change because of you.

Rules every volunteer follows

  • Safety first. Never stand at the platform edge, on tracks, or in anyone's way. Follow every rule of the railway, metro or bus operator and the instructions of their staff and the police.
  • Only kind words. Never push, block, shout at, shame or touch anyone. We remind, we never force.
  • No official powers. Qindiaglobal volunteers and reporters are ordinary citizens. You must never say or suggest that you are from the government, police, railways or the press, and you must not wear anything that looks like an official uniform or ID.
  • Respect privacy. Ask before photographing people up close, never post photos that make fun of anyone, and never post clear faces of children.
  • No politics, religion or money. Keep the message about queues and dignity only. Never collect money in the name of Qindiaglobal.

Qindiaglobal never asks for any fee to apply or to volunteer. If anyone asks you for money in our name, do not pay, and tell us through the form on the home page.
