How to get Google AdSense (step by step)
  1. On your phone, open adsense.google.com and sign in with your Gmail.
  2. Tap Get started. Website: qindiaglobal.com. Choose India, accept the terms.
  3. AdSense shows your Publisher ID (looks like pub-1234567890123456). Copy it, paste it above in Publisher ID, choose Let Google place more ads, and tap Save settings.
  4. Back in AdSense, on "Connect your site", choose the ads.txt snippet method. Our website already serves the file at qindiaglobal.com/ads.txt once you saved the ID. Tap Verify, then Request review.
  5. Google checks the site. This usually takes from a few days up to 2–4 weeks. Keep writing blog posts; sites with more original content are approved more easily.
  6. After approval, in AdSense go to Ads → By ad unit → Display ads, name it "Qindia box", tap Create. Copy the number after data-ad-slot= and paste it above in Ad unit ID. Save.
  7. In AdSense, go to Privacy & messaging and turn on the European regulations message (free). Google needs this for visitors from Europe.
  8. Add your bank details and PAN under Payments. Google pays when your balance reaches ₹8,000 (around the 21st of the month).

Never click your own Google ads or ask people to click them. Google closes accounts for that.
