Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

What we collect

  • When you take the pledge: your city (required), and if you choose to give them, your name, country, and a line for the Pledge wall. We also note the language you used and the date.
  • Email or phone with the pledge: only if you tick the consent box; otherwise it is not saved.
  • When you write to us: what you type in the form (subject, name, city, email or phone, message).
  • For safety: a coded, one-way form of your internet address (a "hash"). It cannot be turned back into the address. We use it only to stop spam and to block people who break these terms.

We do not use advertising, tracking cookies or analytics. The website remembers your language choice and your own pledge link on your phone only (browser storage); this never leaves your device.

What others can see

  • The pledge count and the cities and countries of recent pledges are public.
  • Your own pledge page (qindiaglobal.com/p/…) shows your name, city, country, pledge number and date. It has a private, hard-to-guess link that is not listed on search engines; only people you share it with can see it.
  • If you write a Pledge wall line, it is shown with your first name and city once it passes our checks.
  • Your email, phone number and messages are never shown publicly.

Why we use it

To count and show pledges, to run the Pledge wall, to reply to you, to contact you about this initiative if you agreed to it, and to protect the website from misuse. We never sell, rent or share your details with anyone, except where the law requires us to.

Where it is kept and for how long

Your details are kept on the initiative's own server, which only the owner can access with a password. We keep them while the initiative runs, or until you ask us to remove them.

Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask to see, correct or erase your details, and withdraw your consent to be contacted, at any time. Write to us with the form on the home page and tell us the city and approximate date of your pledge. Children under 18 should take the pledge with a parent's or guardian's help and should not give an email or phone number.

Other services

Photos on the home page are loaded from Unsplash, and the Santali font from Google Fonts; those services can see your internet address when your phone loads them. Share buttons open WhatsApp, Facebook, X or Telegram only when you tap them, under their own privacy policies.
