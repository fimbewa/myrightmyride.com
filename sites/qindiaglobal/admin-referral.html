Referral rewards

Members invite people with qindiaglobal.com/community?ref=their-name. Anyone who reaches the target gets a share of each month's ad income, split by sign-ups. No ad income in a month = nothing to pay. Rules members see: /referral and /legal/referral.

Work out a month

After a month's ad money has reached your bank (from advertisers, and from Google AdSense when Google pays), type the total here. If it was ₹0, type 0: nobody gets anything. You can do it again for the same month to correct it; amounts already paid stay.
