Referral rewards

Invite people to join Q India Community with your own link. Help the movement grow, and once you bring 1,000 real members, share in the website's ad income.

Please read this first

  • Below 1,000 sign-ups there is no reward at all. Only real, active accounts that joined through your link count.
  • After that, each month 10% of the ad income Qindiaglobal actually receives is shared between everyone who has reached 1,000, in proportion to their sign-ups.
  • If there is no ad income in a month, there is no payment for that month. There is no guaranteed income, no fixed or minimum amount, and nothing is made up later.
  • This is a thank-you, not a job or salary. It is free to take part; Qindiaglobal never asks you for money.
  • Never pay people to join, never spam, and never make fake accounts. Fake sign-ups are removed and can cancel your rewards.

Example: in a month the site receives ₹50,000 from ads. 10% = ₹5,000 is the reward pool. Two members qualify: one with 3,000 sign-ups and one with 2,000. They get ₹3,000 and ₹2,000. If that month's ad income were ₹0, both would get ₹0.
